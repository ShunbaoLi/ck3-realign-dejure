[h2]🇨🇳 中文说明[/h2]

[b]写在前面[/b]：
在漫长的 CK3 游玩与各大型历史 Mod（如天朝东亚、拜占庭等）中，由于联姻、继承与征伐，封臣辖地往往犬牙交错、法理四分五裂。
本 Mod 为玩家领主赋予至高无上的[b]“勘定疆理”[/b]皇权敕令：只需在封臣角色上[b]右键交互[/b]，即可一键将该封臣实际统辖的所有非本法理土地更定为其主头衔之法定法理；若封臣为国王且玩家为皇帝，更可将该王国法理直接整合入玩家帝国。

[h3]📌 核心功能矩阵[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]公爵 / 节度使（tier_duchy）[/b]
  - 其直辖或下属伯爵领中存在非本公国法理领地时，右键交互一键更定为该公爵主头衔法理。
  - 彻底终结节度使、公国境内的法理破碎与飞地问题。
[*] [b]国王 / 都护府（tier_kingdom）[/b]
  - 其直辖或下属公国中存在非本王国法理领地时，右键交互一键更定为该王国主头衔法理。
  - 规范四方都护府与藩王疆域，重塑地方大区行政秩序。
[*] [b]皇帝对王国封臣（tier_empire）[/b]
  - 王国封臣的主王国尚未划入领主帝国时，右键交互一键整合为玩家帝国的法定法理。
  - 免去漫长百年的法理漂移，实现真正的大一统敕定。
[/list]

[h2]🌐 English Description[/h2]

[b]Overview[/b]:
In CK3 campaigns and major historical overhauls, vassal borders frequently suffer from messy inheritance, non-de-jure enclaves, and border gore.
[b]CK3 Realign Vassal De Jure[/b grants sovereign rulers the power to issue imperial boundary decrees: right-click any vassal to immediately realign all mismatch lands under their control into the de jure territory of their primary title. When you are an emperor interacting with a king vassal, you can also incorporate their kingdom directly into your imperial de jure borders.

[h3]📌 Feature Matrix[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Dukes / Jiedushi (`tier_duchy`)[/b]
  - Realigns all non-de jure counties held by the duke or their vassals directly into the duke's primary title.
[*] [b]Kings / Protectorates (`tier_kingdom`)[/b]
  - Realigns all non-de jure duchies held by the king or their vassals directly into the king's primary title.
[*] [b]Emperors over King Vassals (`tier_empire`)[/b]
  - Instantly realigns the king's kingdom into your imperial de jure borders without waiting for 100-year drift.
[/list]
